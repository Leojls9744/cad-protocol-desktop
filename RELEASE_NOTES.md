CAD Protocol for Windows opens the live CAD Protocol app (https://cad-dza.caffeine.xyz) in its own window. It's the same app, with the same sign-in, vault and drawings, and it updates by itself.

**New in 1.15: cadprotocol:// links open the app.** Another app or a web page (UnifyField, say) can now open CAD Protocol for Windows with a link: `cadprotocol://open` starts the app, or brings it to the front if it's already open (its sign-in card first, if that's showing). That's all a link can do: nothing else in the address is read, and a link never opens a drawing. The installer registers the links, and the app says so again each time it starts. 1.13 and 1.14 update themselves to 1.15; the links work once that's installed.

**New in 1.14: Sign in opens Internet Identity straight away.** Sign in on the app's card now opens Internet Identity at once in a window of the app's own, over the card, with the CAD Protocol icon: choose your identity, finish with your passkey or Google, and CAD Protocol opens. No page in between and nothing to close; the app remembers your identity, so next time it's Sign in, then Continue. If a sign-in can't be done in the app's window (Google has refused sign-ins inside apps before; a passkey kept by a browser extension lives in that browser), the card's second choice, Sign in with your browser instead, works as 1.13 did. Needs the app's newest web build (v846): until that's published the card is as in 1.13. 1.12 and 1.13 update themselves to 1.14.

**New in 1.13: sign in in a small window.** Sign in with Internet Identity now opens a small window of your own browser right over the sign-in card, instead of a tab in your big browser window. Press Sign in with Internet Identity there, sign in (Internet Identity opens over it), and the small window closes by itself as CAD Protocol opens. Closing the small window yourself cancels the sign-in. It works with Chrome, Edge or Brave as your default browser; with any other the page opens in a tab as before. It's still your own browser, with your passkeys and Google sign-in; the small window has no address bar. With the app's newest web build (v844) Internet Identity opens small too; before that it opens in a tab. 1.11 and 1.12 update themselves to 1.13.

**New in 1.12: a DWG copy beside drawings you save in any folder.** With Auto-backup saves and Companion .dwg on (Settings › Cloud & Geo), each save of a drawing opened from File Explorer, or through the app's Open and Save windows, now also writes a .dwg of the same name beside it, wherever the file is. Before, that only happened inside a folder linked in the app. Needs the app's newest web build (v842). 1.11 updates itself to 1.12.

**New in 1.11.1: .cadp files show the app's icon.** In File Explorer your .cadp drawings now show the CAD Protocol app icon instead of 1.11's document icon. 1.11 updates itself to 1.11.1 (if Explorer still shows the old icon, it changes after a restart).

**New in 1.11: .cadp files open with a double-click.** Drawings you save to this computer (.cadp) now show their own document icon in File Explorer, and double-clicking one opens it in CAD Protocol: the app starts with it, or, if it's already open, it opens there. Ctrl+S saves back to the same file. Open with › CAD Protocol works too. Needs the app's newest web build (v823). 1.10.2 updates itself to 1.11.

**New in 1.10.2: choose from Google Drive in your browser.** Google's file picker can't show in the app's window: it asked you to sign in to Google there, and its Sign in opened the picker in a browser tab where Select did nothing. Now Open from Drive, Choose Default Folder and a project's folder open Google Drive in your own browser: press Continue with Google, choose, and what you chose comes back to the app. Needs the app's newest web build. 1.10.1 updates itself to 1.10.2.

**New in 1.10.1: closing works with the sign-in card.** Closing CAD Protocol signs you out, and in 1.10.0 that could bring the sign-in card up as the app closed and leave it running. Now the card stays away while the app closes and goes with its window. 1.10.0 updates itself to 1.10.1.

**New in 1.10: a sign-in card instead of the full sign-in page.** While CAD Protocol starts you see its loading card as before; then, if you're signed out, a sign-in card of its own (four times the loading card) instead of a full window with the sign-in page. **Sign in with your browser** opens Internet Identity in your own browser: sign in there, come back, and the card shows each step (sign in in your browser, signing you in, opening CAD Protocol), with Cancel while it waits. The app's window opens once you're in. **Use Spatial Canvas offline** opens Spatial Canvas without signing in, where you save drawings as files on this computer. × closes the app. 1.10 also has 1.9's Google Drive and 1.8.2's card fix, and earlier versions update themselves to 1.10.

**New in 1.9: Google Drive.** Settings › Google Drive › Connect Google Drive opens your own browser (Google won't sign in inside an app's window): press Continue with Google there, and you're back in the app connected. Then File › Open from Google Drive opens drawings, DXF, DWG and scans from your Drive, and Save writes the same Drive file again, with a DWG copy beside it. The access Google gives lasts an hour; after that the app asks you to connect again the same way. 1.8 updates itself to 1.9.

**New in 1.8.2: the card shows its picture and words.** The small card that shows while CAD Protocol starts and while it signs you out on close was an empty dark box in the installed app: Windows' installed copy wouldn't show a page straight from the app's own files. The card (and the "You're offline" page) now come from the app's own private address, so they show as they should. Also from 1.8.1: the card appears only once its picture is drawn and stays up long enough to read, and when you close the app its window goes at once and only the small card shows while it saves and signs you out. 1.7 and 1.8 update themselves to 1.8.2.

**New in 1.8: talk in live sessions and meetings.** The app's Hold to talk and meeting mic work in the desktop app: Windows asks once to let it use the microphone. Only sound: the camera and screen stay off.

**New in 1.7: only the small card while it starts.** CAD Protocol now starts as a small card window of its own, with no full window behind it: the app loads out of sight (while it checks for updates, too) and its window opens when it's ready, in its last size or maximized. Closing it while it saves and signs you out shows the same small card instead of the window. 1.6 updates itself to 1.7.

**New in 1.6: a start-up card.** CAD Protocol starts in a small card with its icon and name over its ribbon picture and a bar in the Internet Computer's colors, and the app goes on in the same card while it loads (and shows it as it signs you out when you close the window, instead of the home page). 1.5 updates itself to 1.6.

**New in 1.5: work offline and save to this computer.** In Spatial Canvas, **File › Save to Computer** saves a drawing as a .cadp file in any folder through Windows' own Save window, and **File › Open from Computer** opens one; after that, Save (Ctrl+S) writes the same file. It works online or offline, signed in or not. With the app's newest web build the desktop app also keeps all of CAD Protocol on this computer (about 23 MB, again after each update), so every tool opens offline, and with no connection the sign-in card offers **Work offline**. Files on your computer aren't encrypted, like any drawing file; your vault stays encrypted. 1.4 updates itself to 1.5.

**New in 1.4: a purple title bar.** On Windows 11 the bar across the top of every CAD Protocol window is now CAD Protocol purple with white text, instead of your Windows accent color. Windows 10 keeps its usual bar. 1.3 updates itself to 1.4; nothing to install.

**New in 1.3: it updates itself.** From now on the desktop app keeps itself up to date: when it opens, it checks for a new version and, if there is one, installs it on an update screen and restarts; while it's open it checks every 6 hours, downloads quietly and installs when you restart or close (the badge in the top bar says "Update ready – Restart"). No more running the installer. This is the last version you install by hand: install 1.3 over 1.1; your settings stay.

Also in 1.3: closing the window saves your open drawings to your vault and signs you out, unless you turn that off in Settings › Account and security; with the app's newest web build, every sign-in also signs out after a time without activity (10 minutes unless you change it), with a minute's warning, and a desktop sign-in lasts 8 hours unless you change it.

**New in 1.1: Sign in with your browser.** Google doesn't allow its sign-in inside an app's own window ("This browser or app may not be secure"), so the sign-in card now has **Sign in with your browser**. It opens CAD Protocol in your own browser, where you sign in as usual and press **Continue**. Internet Identity opens once more; pick the same identity. Your browser tab then says you're signed in, and the desktop app comes to the front, signed in to the same account with a session of its own. A passkey (Windows Hello) still works in the desktop app directly: **Sign in here with a passkey**. Installing 1.1 over 1.0 keeps your settings.

What the desktop app adds:

- **Faster pointer while you draft.** While CAD Protocol is in front, the pointer speed is set to 15 on Windows' 1–20 scale, or kept if yours is already faster. Your own speed comes back the moment you switch to another program, minimise, lock the screen or quit. It's never saved to your Windows settings. You can change it or turn it off in the app's Settings.
- **Every key goes to the drawing.** No browser shortcuts get in the way.
- **More room.** No tabs or address bar, and its own window and taskbar icon.

**Installing:** download `CAD-Protocol-Setup.exe` and open it. It installs for your Windows account only, with no admin prompt, and adds Start menu and desktop shortcuts.

**"Windows protected your PC":** the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Before you do, you can check the file is the one built here: run `Get-FileHash .\CAD-Protocol-Setup.exe` in PowerShell and compare the hash with the SHA-256 below.

**Uninstalling:** Settings › Apps › Installed apps › CAD Protocol › Uninstall. Your drawings stay in your vault; nothing is kept on the computer except the window size and pointer setting.
